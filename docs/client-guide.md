# HAV'ARC Field Service — Quick Guide

The app runs in the browser at **https://havarc.vercel.app** and can be installed on a phone like a regular app. It works in the field without a signal: everything is saved on the phone and sent to the office as soon as there is a connection.

## 1. Install on the phone

**iPhone (Safari):** open the link in Safari → tap the Share button (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → Add. The app appears on the home screen with the HAV'ARC icon. Use it from there, not from Safari.

**Android (Chrome):** open the link in Chrome → menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen** (or the "Install app" banner) → Install.

A laptop or desktop browser works as well: same link, no install needed.

## 2. Sign in

Sign in with the email and password you were given. The app stays signed in on the device. **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen sends a reset link by email; the link opens in the browser, set the new password there, then return to the app and sign in.

To change a password: Settings (gear icon, top right) → **Change password**.

## 3. Jobs

The Jobs list shows every service call, newest first, grouped into Today and Earlier. Tap the magnifier to search by work order number, customer or address; the Draft / Completed chips filter the list. Both technicians see the same list.

Badges: **Draft** — started, not finished. **Pending sync** — finished on the phone, not yet sent to the office (no signal). **Completed** — saved, PDFs ready.

## 4. Create a service call (4 steps)

Tap the orange **+** (New job on a laptop).

1. **Service Call & Equipment** — work order number is filled in automatically, date and technician too. Start typing the customer name: known customers appear with their address and any notes on file (gate codes, pets…). Pick the service type and complaint, describe the equipment. **Add second unit** for jobs with more than one unit.
2. **Inspection & Readings** — readings are optional: **Add readings** for maintenance calls, skip for a quick service call. Tick the condition checks.
3. **Findings & Repairs** — tick what was found, what was repaired and what you recommend, add notes and parts used.
4. **Complete Service Call** — final system status (Green / Yellow / Orange / Red), photos (**Add photo** → camera or gallery), customer name and both signatures (sign with a finger on the phone). **Edit invoice** to add line items, tax and discount. **Complete** saves the job.

Every field is saved as you go. Leaving with **Keep draft & exit** keeps the job in the list as a Draft; open it later via ⋯ → **Edit job** to continue.

## 5. Report, invoice and email

A few seconds after **Complete**, the job shows two documents: **Service Report PDF** and **Invoice** with its number (numbers are assigned automatically, in sequence). Tap a document to view it. At the same moment both PDFs are emailed to the office address set in Settings.

**Share PDFs** at the bottom of the job opens the phone's share menu with the PDFs and photos selected — send them by text message, email, WhatsApp or AirDrop.

Editing a completed job (⋯ → **Edit job**, or **Save invoice** after changing the invoice) regenerates the PDFs and sends the email again.

## 6. Working without signal

Keep working as usual. A yellow **Offline** strip appears at the top of the Jobs list; new and edited jobs show **Pending sync**. When the signal is back, the app sends everything by itself while it is open (a blue **Syncing…** strip), and Pending sync turns into Completed. If something could not be sent, a red strip says so — tap it to retry.

If the same job was edited on both phones while offline, the app asks which version to keep.

## 7. Settings

Gear icon on the Jobs list. Company name, contacts and logo (printed on the PDFs), default tax rate, labor rate, the next invoice number and prefix, the invoice footer note, and the office email that receives the PDFs. Changes save automatically.

## 8. Good to know

- Photos are compressed on the phone before sending, so a job with several photos uploads quickly.
- The app updates itself: when a new version is released it reloads on the next open.
- Deleting a job (⋯ → **Delete job**) removes it, its photos and PDFs on all devices. There is no undo.
